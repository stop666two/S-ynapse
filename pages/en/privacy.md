# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 19, 2026**

We value your privacy. This policy explains how this blog collects, uses and protects your personal information.

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide

When you contact us via the comment system, contact form or email, we may collect:

- User name or nickname
- Email address
- Comment content

### Information collected automatically

This blog is a purely static site hosted on Cloudflare's global network and keeps no server access logs. However, third-party services we use may collect:

- **Page analytics**: anonymized visit counts and page views
- **Browser information**: browser type, operating system, screen resolution (for display optimization)
- **Referrers**: the site you arrived from

## How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

- Reply to your comments and inquiries
- Improve content and user experience
- Understand content popularity

## Cookies

This blog itself does not use cookies. The following third-party services may:

| Service | Purpose | Cookie type |
|------|------|------------|
| Cloudflare | Network security & acceleration | Necessary |
| Google Fonts | Font loading | Functional |
| Giscus / Disqus | Comment system (if enabled) | Functional |

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Third-Party Services

- **Cloudflare** — CDN acceleration and DNS, [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **Google Fonts** — web font loading, [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **GitHub** — code hosting and Issues comments, [privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/privacy)
- **jsdelivr** — CDN asset delivery, [privacy policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy)

## Data Storage & Transfer

Your comment data is stored on GitHub servers and may be transferred across borders. Submitting a comment means you agree to this transfer.

## Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data:

- HTTPS encryption site-wide
- CSP to prevent XSS attacks
- External-link warning mechanism
- Subresource Integrity (SRI) verification for static assets

## Your Rights

You have the right to know how your information is processed, to request deletion of your comment data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us via the details below to exercise these rights.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Please check back periodically.
